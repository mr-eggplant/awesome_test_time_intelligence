# Contributing

[← Overview](README.md) · [All papers](ALL_PAPERS.md)

Thank you for helping us keep this collection accurate and up to date. We welcome paper suggestions, official implementations, classification ideas, and corrections.

## Suggest a paper

Please open a [paper suggestion](https://github.com/mr-eggplant/awesome_test_time_intelligence/issues/new?template=paper-suggestion.yml) with:

- **Paper metadata:** title, authors, year, venue or publication status, and an official paper link.
- **Method and code:** the method name used by the authors and an official implementation link (optional).
- **Suggested placement:** the most suitable topic page, section, and subsection.
- **New classification (optional):** if the paper does not fit the current taxonomy, propose a new classification with a brief explanation.

This collection focuses on work that directly studies test-time learning, test-time scaling, their interaction, or applications of these mechanisms.

## Report a correction

We also appreciate corrections to metadata, links, and classifications. Please open a [correction report](https://github.com/mr-eggplant/awesome_test_time_intelligence/issues/new?template=correction.yml) with the affected page or entry, the corrected information, and a supporting source. If the issue concerns classification, suggests a more suitable existing location or a clearer new classification.
